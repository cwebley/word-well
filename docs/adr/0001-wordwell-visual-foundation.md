# WordWell visual foundation

WordWell will use a hand-rolled SwatchKit app with HTML, vanilla JavaScript, and CSS. Its type pairing is Literata for Word lesson reading and Instrument Sans for interface text. Waterline is the theme: water is the application shell and link color; wood is the primary action color; moss is the quiet supporting color. The three role hues share `oklch(45% 0.075 hue)` so they keep a similar visual strength while their hues differ.
